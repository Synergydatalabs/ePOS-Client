Help & FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can’t find what you need? Email support@indianbeans.com and a person will reply, usually within one business day.

Getting started

How long does setup take?

Most small teams are up and running the same day. Import a spreadsheet, choose a pipeline template and invite your colleagues.

Can you move our data from another CRM?

Yes. We import from spreadsheets and most popular CRMs. Migration is free and assisted on Growth, and done for you on Scale.

Is there a mobile app?

Indian Beans works in the browser on phones and tablets, so your team can update deals between meetings.

Who is Indian Beans for?

Businesses that sell to other businesses — agencies, software companies, wholesalers, manufacturers and consultancies — typically with 2 to 200 people in sales and account management.

Billing

Do I need a card for the free trial?

No. You get 14 days of full access to any plan. You only add payment details if you decide to keep going.

How does per-user pricing work?

You pay for each person who logs in to your account. Add or remove users at any time; changes are prorated on your next invoice.

Can I switch plans?

Yes. Upgrades take effect straight away and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at your next billing date.

What currency and taxes apply?

All prices are in US dollars and exclude VAT or sales tax, which is added where required. Your bank may charge a currency conversion fee.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes. There’s a 30-day money-back guarantee on your first payment. Full details are in our Refund Policy.

Data & security

Who owns the data?

You do. You can export everything to CSV at any time. We never sell your data.

Where is my data stored?

On secure cloud servers in the UK and EU, encrypted at rest and in transit. See our Security page.

Are you GDPR compliant?

We follow UK GDPR and act as your data processor. A Data Processing Agreement is available on request.
