Security

Your customer data is your business. We treat it that way.

A CRM holds your most valuable relationships. Here’s how we keep that information safe.

Encrypted everywhere

Data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2+ and at rest with AES-256.

Reliable hosting

We run on established cloud infrastructure with servers in the UK and EU, and daily encrypted backups.

Secure sign-in

Two-factor authentication on all plans, Google sign-in, and SAML single sign-on on Scale.

Access you control

Roles and permissions decide who can view, edit, export or delete. Admins can remove access instantly.

Audit trail

See who changed what and when. Full audit logs on Scale.

UK GDPR

We act as your data processor, offer a Data Processing Agreement, and help you answer data subject requests.

How we work

  • Staff access to customer data is limited to what’s needed for support, and only with your permission.
  • We never sell customer data or use it for advertising.
  • You can export all your data at any time, and we delete it after you close your account (see our Privacy Policy).
  • Security updates are applied promptly, and we review our dependencies continuously.

Report a vulnerability

If you believe you’ve found a security issue, email security@indianbeans.com. Please give us reasonable time to fix it before sharing details publicly. We’ll acknowledge reports within two business days.

Need a Data Processing Agreement or a security questionnaire completed? Email legal@indianbeans.com.
