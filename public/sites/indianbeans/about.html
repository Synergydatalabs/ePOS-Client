About us

Good relationships, like good coffee, take care at every stage.

We’re a UK company building the CRM we wanted to use ourselves: simple to start, fair to pay for, and serious about your data.

Why we started

Indian Beans was founded by Ahmad Hassan after years spent watching B2B teams struggle with their customer software. Small companies were stuck in spreadsheets. Growing ones bought enterprise CRMs, spent months configuring them, and then watched their sales team quietly go back to their inboxes.

We set out to build the middle ground: a CRM a team can learn in an afternoon, that grows with the business, and that shows its prices on the website.

Why “Indian Beans”

India grows some of the world’s most carefully tended coffee. On hillside estates, every cherry is picked by hand when it’s ready — not before, not after. Then it’s dried, roasted and brewed, each step with its own craft.

B2B sales works the same way. A lead becomes a customer through the right attention at the right moment. That’s the job our software does, and it’s why our sample pipeline goes from cherry to brewed.
