Contacts & accounts

People and companies

Link contacts to companies, track roles like decision-maker or budget holder, and see the full buying group.

Import and de-duplicate

Upload a CSV or Excel file. Duplicates are found and merged before they clutter your data.

Custom fields and tags

Add the fields your business needs — industry, contract end date, account tier — and filter by any of them.

Communication

Two-way email sync

Connect Gmail or Microsoft 365. Emails with customers are logged on the right record automatically.

Meeting scheduler

Share a booking link that checks your calendar. Booked meetings appear on the deal.

Call logging

Log calls in two clicks with outcome and notes, or connect your phone system through the API.

Insight & control

Reports and dashboards

Win rate, pipeline value, sales cycle and activity by person or team, ready out of the box.

Forecasting

Weighted forecasts by month and quarter, with target tracking for each rep (Scale).

Roles and permissions

Choose who can see, edit, export or delete records. Field-level control on Scale.

Everywhere you work

Web and mobile

Your data, exportable

Export any list or your whole account to CSV at any time. No lock-in.
