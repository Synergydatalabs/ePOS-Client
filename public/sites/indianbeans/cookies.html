Cookie Policy

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. This policy explains which ones we use.

This website

Our marketing website does not set any cookies of its own and does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver them.

The Indian Beans app

When you log in to the Service we use strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in, remember your settings and protect against security threats. These cookies are required for the Service to work, so they don’t need consent.

Checkout & payment cookies

Payments are processed by Paddle, who acts as our Merchant of Record. When you reach the checkout, Paddle’s hosted overlay sets strictly necessary cookies on its own domain (checkout.paddle.com) to secure the session, remember your progress through the payment, and prevent fraud. These are first-party to Paddle, not to Indian Beans, and are set only after you reach the checkout.

If you choose to pay with KakaoPay, you are briefly redirected to Kakao Pay Corp.’s own domain (kakaopay.com) to authorise the payment with your KakaoPay app. Kakao Pay sets its own session cookies to complete the authorisation; these are governed by Kakao Pay’s own cookie notice. Once the payment settles you are returned here and those cookies no longer apply.

Similar behaviour applies to other payment methods available via Paddle (card, PayPal, Alipay, Apple Pay, Google Pay, iDEAL and others). Each provider’s own cookies are governed by that provider’s cookie policy, which Paddle links to from the checkout page.

Full details of Paddle’s cookies are at paddle.com/legal/cookies.

Analytics

If we add analytics in future, we will ask for your consent before setting any non-essential cookies and update this page.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies will stop you from logging in to the Service, and blocking checkout cookies will stop you from completing a payment.
