Billing questions

Do I need a card for the free trial?

No. You get 14 days of full access to any plan. You only add payment details if you decide to keep going.

How does per-user pricing work?

You pay for each person who logs in to your account. Add or remove users at any time; changes are prorated on your next invoice.

Can I switch plans?

Yes. Upgrades take effect straight away and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at your next billing date.

What currency and taxes apply?

All prices are in US dollars and exclude VAT or sales tax, which is added where required. Your bank may charge a currency conversion fee.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes. There’s a 30-day money-back guarantee on your first payment. Full details are in our Refund Policy.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Your account stays active until the end of the period you’ve paid for. You can export everything, and we keep your data for 30 days afterwards in case you return.

Do you offer discounts?

Annual billing saves 20%. We also offer volume pricing for 50+ users and discounts for registered charities — contact sales.
