Three ways to share a link with a customer

Option 1 · Email

Paste the URL into an email. Our Mail app automatically turns it into a clickable link. Customer clicks → pay page opens → they enter card → done.

Option 2 · WhatsApp / SMS

Send the URL in a chat. On mobile, the link opens directly in the phone's browser.

Option 3 · QR Code

Generate a QR code of the URL (free: qr-code-generator.com or built into most POS printers). Print it and display at your counter. Customer scans with their phone camera → pay page opens → done.

What the customer sees

  1. They open your Indian Beans checkout page (gold banner, your logo at the top).
  2. They see the plan name, price, and "Then $X.XX every month" subscription schedule.
  3. They type their email + name, accept your Terms, and click Pay with card.
  4. Card entry appears — they type card number, expiry, CVV, postal code.
  5. Payment confirmed. They see a thank-you message. The receipt is emailed to them immediately.
  6. The subscription is active. Their card will be charged automatically every month until they cancel.
Secure by design: Card details are entered directly into our payment processor's secure form — they never touch Indian Beans servers. We're PCI-compliant through our processor.

Creating a new payment link

Not just subscriptions — you can create one-time payment links for individual orders too (e.g. "pay for your custom coffee bean order"). Here's how:

  1. Dashboard → Payment Links → Create New Link button
  2. Fill in:
    • Link name — for your records only (customers don't see this)
    • Mode — one-time or subscription
    • If subscription: pick an interval (monthly, yearly, etc.)
    • Price + currency
    • Short URL slug — customize the end of the link (e.g. deluxe-blend → indianbeans.com/l/deluxe-blend). Must be unique.
  3. Save → copy the generated URL → share it anywhere.

Refunding a payment

  1. Invoices → click the paid invoice
  2. Refund button (top-right)
  3. Full refund or partial — enter the amount
  4. Confirm. Customer sees the refund on their statement within 5–10 business days.

Cancelling a subscription

If a customer asks you to cancel their subscription:

  1. Invoices → find any paid invoice from that customer
  2. Open it → Cancel Subscription button
  3. Confirm. The customer will not be billed on the next cycle. All past payments remain.

Troubleshooting

If this happens... Try this
Customer says "page didn't load" Check the URL has no typos. Each link is case-sensitive. Try opening it yourself in an incognito tab.
"Awaiting payment" never changes to "Paid" Payment can take up to 30 seconds to settle. If still "Awaiting" after 2 minutes, check the invoice detail — it will show the exact error.
Customer says card was declined Normal. Ask them to try a different card or contact their bank. The invoice stays "Sent" so they can retry on the same link.
Can't log in to the dashboard Make sure you're using indianbeans.com/partner/login (not /signin, that's for a different app). Password is case-sensitive.
